# Axiom EDA — Professional Logo Design & Brand Identity Manual

## 1. Brand Essence & Design Philosophy

**Axiom** is an aerospace-grade, high-performance Hardware Description Language (HDL) simulation, elaboration, and analysis engine built natively in Rust. It replaces legacy, multi-gigabyte bloat (e.g., AMD Vivado, Synopsys VCS, Cadence Incisive) with millisecond in-RAM Cranelift JIT compilation, stratified event queue visibility, zero-time delta-cycle introspection, and physics-informed silicon telemetry.

### Core Visual Metaphors:
1. **The Axiom "A" & Greek Delta ($\Delta$) Fusion**:
   - The Greek letter Delta represents the $\delta$-cycle: the zero-time discrete simulation step where combinational logic settles before simulation time advances.
   - The capital "A" symbolizes Axiom, First Principles, and aerospace precision.
   - A unified glyph fusing the ascending delta blade with an authoritative chevron or apex conveys upward thrust, hypersonic aerodynamics, and mathematical certainty.
2. **Discrete Multi-State Waveform Transition**:
   - Digital electronics operates on discrete transitions: rising edge, falling edge, setup time, and hold time.
   - The logo should reflect clean rectilinear or angular step transitions rather than soft, organic, or wavy curves.
3. **Silicon Crystal Lattice & Netlist Primitives**:
   - Modern semiconductor dies (FinFET, GAAFET, 3nm/5nm silicon) are organized as crystalline arrays of logic slices, lookup tables (LUTs), and interconnect routing switches.
   - Subtle geometric faceting or crystalline angles convey microelectronic hardware without resorting to cliche DIP-chip pins or crude circuit board squiggles.
4. **Pure Speed & In-RAM Compilation**:
   - Cranelift JIT compiles directly into machine code in milliseconds in RAM. The visual form must convey velocity, precision, and sharpness — razor edges, chamfered corners, and high directional tension.

## 2. Strict Design Constraints & Anti-Patterns

### Anti-Patterns (Immediate Disqualification):
- **NO Literal Clichés**: Do not draw green printed circuit boards (PCBs), caterpillar dual-in-line (DIP) IC chips with metal legs, solder pads, lightning bolts, or lightbulbs.
- **NO Generic Vivado / EDA Clones**: Do not draw spinning vortexes, swirling spirals, generic atom models, or amorphous gradient blobs.
- **NO Visual Noise**: Avoid microscopic circuit tracks, tiny decorative vias, dense grids, or hair-thin lines that dissolve into blurry sludge at 16x16 or 32x32 pixels.
- **NO Cartoonish Skew**: Avoid bubbly round corners, heavy comic drop shadows, skeuomorphic bevels, or casual hand-drawn flourishes.
- **NO Emojis**: Strictly zero emojis anywhere in assets, documentation, or code.

### Required Aesthetic Attributes:
- **Scalability**: Flawless legibility and visual weight from 16x16 favicon to 1024x1024 app icon.
- **Dark Theme Authority**: Designed natively on obsidian background (`#070a12`), radiating an engineering instruments feel (radar HUD, logic analyzer, telemetry cockpit).
- **Mathematical Precision**: Governed by clean angles ($30^\circ$, $45^\circ$, $60^\circ$, or golden ratio $\phi \approx 1.618$), deliberate stroke weights, and pixel-snapped coordinates.
- **Minimalist Silhouette**: The glyph should be instantly recognizable as a single-color silhouette (monochrome), while coming alive with modern cyan/cobalt/purple luminescence in full color.

## 4. Technical SVG Vector Standards

1. **Coordinate System**:
   - Square viewBox: `viewBox="0 0 512 512"` (or `0 0 256 256` for compact variants).
   - Centering: Optical center positioned precisely at `(256, 260)` to account for the visual upward buoyancy of triangular/delta glyphs.
2. **Stroke & Geometry Rules**:
   - When strokes are used: `stroke-linecap="round"` or `stroke-linecap="square"` with explicit `stroke-linejoin="round"` or `"miter"`.
   - Polygons and paths must have clean, non-fractional coordinates snapped to integer grid or 0.5 grid.
   - Closed paths must use proper `d="..."` commands with zero redundant control points.
3. **Gradients & Filters**:
   - All gradients defined in `<defs>` with semantic IDs (e.g., `axiomCyanGradient`, `axiomCobaltFacet`, `axiomGlowFilter`).
   - Use linear and radial gradients with smooth color stops; avoid harsh banding.
   - Glow effects must be subtle SVG drop-shadows or dual-stroked luminous paths, never heavy blur masks that cause rendering lag in web browsers.

## 5. Harsh Critic Evaluation Rubric (6 Dimensions, 1 to 10 Points)

The `axiom_logo_critic` must evaluate every candidate design against this exact 6-dimension rubric.
**Passing Standard: A candidate is ONLY accepted if it scores $\ge 9$ in EVERY category (Minimum Total: 54 / 60).**

### Criterion 1: Brand Alignment & Metaphor (1 - 10)
- **10**: Masterful integration of the Axiom "A", the Greek $\Delta$ (delta cycle), and digital pulse transition. Radiates aerospace engineering rigor.
- **7-8**: Recognizable "A" or delta shape, but feels like generic tech or aerospace rather than EDA/silicon simulation.
- **4-6**: Vague geometric shape with no obvious connection to hardware, EDA, or simulation.
- **1-3**: Cliche PCB tracks, cartoonish lightning, or generic copycat.

### Criterion 2: Geometric Precision & Balance (1 - 10)
- **10**: Flawless optical balance, golden-ratio proportions, deliberate angles ($30^\circ$, $60^\circ$ or $45^\circ$), harmonious negative space.
- **7-8**: Good overall geometry but minor optical weight imbalance or slightly cramped negative space.
- **4-6**: Awkward angles, misaligned vertices, or visual lopsidedness.
- **1-3**: Cluttered, sloppy vertices, haphazard asymmetry.

### Criterion 3: Scalability & Legibility (1 - 10)
- **10**: Instantaneous recognition at 16x16, 24x24, 32x32, 64x64, and 512x512. Silhouette remains starkly legible even when reduced to favicon size.
- **7-8**: Clear at 64x64+, but minor loss of internal detail at 16x16.
- **4-6**: Internal details turn into mud at small sizes; requires squinting.
- **1-3**: Completely indistinguishable smudge below 48x48.

### Criterion 4: Color Palette & Dark Obsidian Harmony (1 - 10)
- **10**: Stunning obsidian, electric cyan, hyper blue, and neon purple palette. Luminescent without being garish; high contrast on dark backgrounds.
- **7-8**: Good colors, but slightly too saturated or lacking depth contrast between adjacent facets.
- **4-6**: Muddy or washed-out gradients; low contrast against dark backgrounds.
- **1-3**: Clashing colors, rainbow garishness, or illegible on dark background.

### Criterion 5: Production SVG Craft & Cleanliness (1 - 10)
- **10**: Pristine, semantic SVG code. Well-structured `<defs>`, scalable viewBox, integer-aligned coordinates, zero bloat, valid XML.
- **7-8**: Valid SVG, but contains slight coordinate redundancies or minor path complexity.
- **4-6**: Sloppy path definitions, excessive inline styles, or poorly constructed gradients.
- **1-3**: Broken SVG syntax, raster embeds, or non-scalable absolute units.

### Criterion 6: Distinction & Memorability (1 - 10)
- **10**: Highly iconic, singular silhouette. Stands proudly alongside Apple Silicon M-series, Rust, Linear, and Vercel marks while having an unmistakable EDA identity.
- **7-8**: Attractive modern icon, but resembles existing developer tools slightly.
- **4-6**: Generic geometric triangle/chevron seen in dozens of templates.
- **1-3**: Derivative, forgettable, or amateurish.
